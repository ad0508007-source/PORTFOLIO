#water-ai 2026
AI-Based Water Analysis System

An AI-based water analysis system designed to detect potential microplastic particles and evaluate water quality using computer vision and sensor data. The system captures water-sample images through a smartphone camera, processes the visual data using Python, OpenCV, and TensorFlow, and combines it with turbidity measurements collected through an ESP32-based monitoring unit. The analyzed results are transmitted to the connected hardware and displayed through a 16×2 I2C LCD with LED indicators, providing a real-time and accessible approach to water-quality monitoring.
